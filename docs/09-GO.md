## 2. Why this wins — the claims, all measured, all survivable

**Read `docs/11-honest-headline.md` before this section. It is the source of truth for every number
here and nothing in this file may exceed it.** Measured live against `jev-1.13.0` on 2026-09-20;
every figure is the modal result over **five full repeats**, not one run. Reproduce with
`python scripts/engine_decontaminated_v2.py` (`DECON_REPEATS=5`). Raw:
`data/decontaminated-results.json` · `data/standard-extracted.json` · both judge states, printed
for a hostile reader, in `data/decontaminated-judge-states.json`.

### Claim 1 — her standard is recovered, not authored, and it survives a held-out test

Three of five review personas **opened our code** and found hand-written rules (*"Fewer steps beats
more steps"*, *"She does NOT spend the whole budget"*) inside Maya's judge state, while the
dermatologist's state had no budget rule at all. **They were right.** We rebuilt it, twice.

In `scripts/engine_decontaminated_v2.py` Maya's standard is extracted from her public posts through
**fourteen typed slots**; the dermatologist's standard is extracted through **the same fourteen
slots** from clinical guidance; both judge states contain nothing but those extracted slots plus a
shelf that is **byte-identical on both sides**. No hand-written judgement rule survives anywhere —
grep the states for `Fewer steps`, `does NOT spend`, `whole budget`, `Non-negotiable`: zero hits.

| | Measured | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Typed slots, asked of both sides | **14** | `data/standard-extracted.json` |
| Extraction time, both standards | **0.65 s** | `11-honest-headline.md` §1 |
| Slots they **agree** on | **10** | §1, the fourteen questions |
| Slots they **disagree** on | **4** | §1, the fourteen questions |
| Basket stability over 5 repeats | **1.00** | §1 |
| Her private routing sheet recovered from public posts alone | **3 of 3** | §1, held out |

**The held-out check is the headline.** `data/case-001-maya.json → decision_rules.routing`
(**E-02.2** — "the routing she already does in her head") is **never put in any prompt**. It is
loaded at the very end, only to score what we pulled out of her public captions plus the shelf:

| Condition | Her private sheet | Discovered from public posts alone | |
|---|---|---|---|
| DRY | Cloud Cream / Barrier Oil | **Cloud Cream** | ✓ |
| OILY | Daily Gel | **Daily Gel** | ✓ |
| REDNESS | Red Reset | **Red Reset** | ✓ |

**3 / 3.** It is in the code and a judge can grep `HELD_OUT_PRIVATE_ROUTING` to confirm it only
ever touches the scorer. This is the one claim in the repo that a re-run cannot soften, because the
answer sheet was written by Maya and sealed before the test.

> **"We asked her public posts and a dermatologist's clinical guidance the same fourteen questions
> and had both answers back in under a second. They agree on ten and disagree on four. Then we
> checked the routing we'd pulled out of her captions against the routing sheet Maya keeps in her
> head — which this machine has never seen — and it matched three times out of three."**

### Claim 1a — the divergence story is dead. **0/8.** We say it first

With both judges asked the same fourteen questions, Maya and the dermatologist return the
**identical basket for all eight shoppers**.

| | Authored (what we nearly claimed) | Decontaminated v1, 6 slots | **Expanded v2, 14 slots** |
|---|---|---|---|
| Basket divergence | 5/6 shoppers | 3/8 shoppers | **0 of 8 shoppers** |
| Fewer products | 0.7 | 0.2 | **0.00** |
| Less money | £14 | £7 | **£0** |

**Every figure in the first two columns is retired.** Verified across **ten full sweeps** and
**four configurations** — matched derm corpus, narrow derm corpus, objective shelf, v1 shelf.
Per-run divergence: `[0, 0, 0, 0, 0]`.

**Why this is honest, and why we volunteer it.** The 3/8 was not a small finding, it was
under-specification: ask both sides six questions and you measure the gap in your own question set;
ask fourteen and the gap goes to zero. We were told to publish whatever number came out. It came
out zero and we are not rounding it toward the story. We also say it **before** we are asked,
because a judge who reads this repo finds it in ninety seconds, and finding it *after* we have
claimed a saving costs us everything else in the submission.

The volunteered follow-up, said the moment baskets or money come up:

> **"On the actual baskets, they land in the same place eight times out of eight. Once you ask both
> sides enough questions, the dermatologist and the creator buy the same things. The difference is
> who it's for and how it's explained — not a cheaper bill."**

**⛔ Retired — never a live claim again, in any document, on any slide, on stage:**
`£14` · `£7` · `0.7 fewer products` · `0.2 fewer products` · `5/6 shoppers diverge` ·
`3/8 shoppers diverge` · *"a dermatologist says three products and seventy-two"* ·
*"forty-six pounds this machine just talked her out of spending"* · any *"Maya saves you money
versus a dermatologist"* framing at all. They exist in this repo only inside lists like this one.
Also **off every slide:** `skippable_category` — it flipped between `makeup` and `serum` across
five identical extractions (stability 0.60).

### Claim 1b — ranking is generic; routing and refusal are not
On **ranking products**, Maya and a dermatologist were 85% identical (corr +0.86). That is why we
do not rank. And on **choosing the set** they now agree too — 0/8. What is *not* generic is whose
routing fires and what gets declined: her routing matched her own sealed sheet **3/3**, and the £62
refusal (*"Good. Not £62 good."*, **E-04.6**) is hers and nobody else's. **Taste is visible in the
refusals, not in the totals.**

### Claim 2 — it routes like her, on a shopper it has never seen
**Priya says her face freaks out at everything. She gets Red Reset — 5 runs out of 5, with nothing
forced.** That is Mode A: her routing sits in `state`, no feasibility filter, no rule written by us.
Redness → Red Reset came out of a caption, and it agrees with the routing sheet in her head.

**Caveat, written down rather than hidden, because the whole result rests on it:** `route_reacting`
for Maya is **low-confidence — 0.34**. It is stable (`Red Reset` in 5/5 extractions); the low number
is the model genuinely torn between `Red Reset` and *"tell her to buy nothing"*, which is exactly
the tension in her caption. Honest, stable, low. Do not present it as certainty.

### Claim 3 — "It answers all twelve, not two"
All 12 exhibits from E-01, one request each, **0.68s total**. 11 at high confidence; E-01.11
(*"not a beauty question but what would you do if it was your money?"*) comes back at **0.25** and is
**correctly held for Maya**. Low confidence is a feature, not a miss.

And it asks her questions back, in her words: shade → *"What are you using now?"*; first date →
*"Do you actually care about skincare or do you just want to look hot tomorrow?"*

### Claim 4 — "It makes her work leverage, not a machine"
78% handled without her · 8% held · **14% refused and referred on.** The safety gate caught every
medical trap we planted — tretinoin, pregnancy, accutane, perioral dermatitis, eczema medication,
a 12-year-old — via a `noul` against her own stated scope, not a keyword list.

> **70 hours a month → ~12 cards a day.**

## 3. The product — THREE URLs, nothing else

Freeze the build to exactly three route shapes. Anything not on this list is not built.

### `/maya` — THE MORNING APPROVAL QUEUE ← *Maya's #1 ask, in both reviews*
Her overnight DMs as a stack of cards. Each carries the drafted reply, its confidence, and three
buttons: **send · edit · hold**. Anything under the confidence gate is pre-held. Anything medical is
pre-refused and referred on. Every edit writes back a correction that moves her standard.
Header: `you approved 30 replies in 4 minutes`.

> **It never sends. It drafts.** Say that sentence on stage. Maya scored `would_i_use_it` **6/10**
> with the note *"v2 does not contain the word approve — who presses send? If it sends by itself
> I am terrified."* This screen is the answer, and `engine_overnight_queue.py` already does the
> triage underneath it.

The four-way split (**answered · asked back · held · referred on**) *is* the hero visual. Honest,
fast, legible at 3 metres, and obviously about a person's day — unlike a 359-tile grid.
Live counter: `50 DMs · 250 judgments · 0.85s · 78% handled`.

### `/ask` — THE PUBLIC LINK (goes in her bio; this is the audience's side)
**Three chip taps, no text box.** Skin ×5 · budget ×3 · how many (1 / 2 / a few). Card in under six
seconds. A free-text box exists as a fallback and routes through `engine_dm_router.py`.

This is the fix for `would_my_audience_use_it` (6.6). It also finally serves **Alex** — the silent
browser who is 61% of buyers and who will never send a DM. She does not have to become a different
person; she taps three chips.

### `/c/<id>` — THE VERDICT CARD (and the share)
Phone-first. **Build the share image first**, and make the card a rendering of the same component,
so there is no code path where the card exists and the image does not. One 1080×1350 `og:image` per
card id so it previews inside iMessage and WhatsApp.

Contains: the person and what they asked · **Maya's basket**, the total, and *what she left unspent*
· **what she left out and why**, always with the alternative · her ceiling as a **band**
(`about £50–55`), never a decimal · provenance (`estimated from Maya's own posts — she sets the
line`) and the exhibit ref · **the money line** (affiliate status per product + running basket value).

**Re-decide on open.** No scheduler, no price-watch service, no fake clock: the card simply
re-enumerates every time it is opened. That delivers the payday beat for free.
**"Do it for me instead"** on a received card re-runs the basket with the receiver's constraints and
mints a child card carrying `parent_card_id` — which is how the private forward becomes observable.

### Surface B — THE VERDICT CARD (click any card; this is the audience's side)
Phone-shaped. Permalink. Share image. Contains:
- The person and what they asked (named, per the brief)
- **Maya's basket** — the set, the total, and *what she left unspent*
- **What she left out and why** — the refusal, always with the alternative
- **Her ceiling** vs the sticker price, as a **band** (`about £50–55`), never a decimal
- Provenance: `Estimated from Maya's own posts — she sets the line` + the exhibit ref (`E-04.6`)
- A drag handle on the line. Drag it → everything re-ranks instantly, zero inference.

### Surface C — THE HANDOFF (the share, and the 16:00 answer)
Forward the card. The receiver has never heard of Maya. **The card re-decides for them.**
Same card → sister (oily, £40) gets SPF 50 £26; mum (dry, £100) gets £64 and keeps £36;
a colleague who owns SPF gets Daily Gel £24 and is not re-sold the SPF.

Every card carries `card_id` + `parent_card_id`, so the private forward becomes **observable** —
that is the 41% dark-social gap from E-09.4, instrumented.

## 4. Onboarding — 90 seconds, no forms

1. **0:00** Paste handle. No signup, no account, no quiz.
2. **0:01** *"Here's what I think I heard"* — her standard, extracted from her public posts
   (**measured 0.67s, 89% correct**), each trait shown **next to the caption it came from**.
   She corrects, she doesn't author.
3. **0:30** *The wow must land here.* A verdict on a real DM she has never answered.
4. **0:45** Optional 60-second voice pass for what a scrape cannot see — her private intake rules.
   (Verified: 12s of speech → typed verdicts across her whole shelf in 1.4s.)
5. **1:30** Her inbox lights up.

**Time to first value: under 90 seconds. The first thing she sees is a verdict, not a form.**

## 5. Four known bugs — fix these before anything else

1. **Skin type must be multi-label.** A single `choice` loses *"dry **+** redness"* — measured: it
   returned Cloud Cream and never fired REDNESS → Red Reset. **Use one `noul` per condition**
   (dry / oily / combination / sensitive / redness), not one `choice`. Her routing is multi-condition.
2. **Always use the two-stage basket.** Score all baskets → take top 8 → one `choice`. The
   single-stage version gave a different top basket between runs. Two-stage was stable.
3. **Routing rules must be structured data** in `state` (`her_written_routing_rules`), not prose.
   As prose they did not fire.
4. **Criteria must describe concrete situations.** `later` vs `not_a_question` overlapped and
   misrouted "buying it payday" at 0.65. Rewriting it as *"waiting for money or time — payday, next
   month, when it restocks"* took it to **0.96**.

## 7. The 90 seconds

**Open cold on a refusal, not on a proof.** A real message and a machine declining to sell is a
stronger first five seconds than any counter.

1. Read @jessica's real DM aloud (E-01.5): **"I already have the night serum. Do I need the barrier
   cream too?"** → the card comes back **"No. You don't."** → then point at what is *not* in it:
   **"And the sixty-two pound serum isn't in there either. Maya rates that one eight out of ten.
   Her own words: 'Good. Not sixty-two quid good.'"** → **"It just turned down the sale."**
   (**E-04.6**, verbatim from the case pack — evidence, not a computed number, so a re-run cannot
   falsify it. Per `docs/research/tano-and-judges-intel.md` §5.4, declining the sale is the
   strongest trust proof available in this room and nobody else will show one.)
   **⛔ Never speak a basket total, a dermatologist comparison or a saving in this beat.**
2. **"Maya gets four thousand eight hundred of those a month. She answers eighteen hundred.
   The rest get silence."**
3. Paste her handle. **"Fourteen typed questions — her captions on one side, a dermatologist's
   clinical guidance on the other. Both back in under a second."** Show the extracted table, each
   row next to the caption it came from: **"They agree on ten. They disagree on four."** Then
   volunteer the 0/8 before anyone can find it: **"And before anyone asks — the baskets come out
   identical. Eight shoppers out of eight."** Then the held-out test, which is the real claim:
   **"Maya keeps a routing sheet in her head. This machine never saw it. It matched three out of
   three."** (Keep `skippable_category` off the screen — stability 0.60.)
4. `/maya` floods. Cards resolve four ways. Counter runs. **"Twelve real messages, point-six-eight
   seconds. Seventy-eight per cent handled."**
5. Point at the held card: **"This one it refused. It wants Maya."** Point at the red one:
   **"This one said 'I'm pregnant.' It won't touch it — and it never sends anything. It drafts."**
6. Open Priya's card: **"Priya says her face freaks out at everything. She gets Red Reset — five
   runs out of five, and nobody wrote that rule. It came out of a caption, and it agrees with the
   routing sheet in her head."** *(The £62 refusal has already been spent in beat 1 — do not repeat
   it here. If asked how sure the machine is: `route_reacting` is stable but low-confidence at
   0.34, and we say so.)*
7. Forward the card. **"Her sister opens it. Oily skin, forty quid."** → it re-decides.
   **"Same card. Different answer. That's the forty-one per cent nobody can see."**
8. Flip a switch on `/onboard`. **"And we don't claim to know her standard. She does."**
   The whole queue re-ranks. *(If Maya is in the room, she flips it.)*
9. **"Seventy hours a month becomes twelve cards a day."**

**Final line:** *"Maya no longer has to be awake for her audience to get her answer —
because her judgement now runs without her."*

## 9. Non-negotiables

- **Never** show a float score or a decimal pound to anyone. Bands: `about £50–55`.
- **Never** put first-person Maya words on a product she hasn't touched. Third person, labelled.
- **Never** publicly stamp a named brand negative. Private to the asker; blocklist her contracted
  brands. **Check the wall against Tano's clients (Skin+Me, Bloom & Wild) before 19:45.**
- **Never** say "autonomous", "AI", "clone", or "chatbot". Say **leverage**, **her standard**, **calibrated**.
- **Never** claim a saving, a smaller basket or a cheaper bill versus a dermatologist. Our own
  symmetric test returns **0/8 divergence, 0.00 products, £0**. The retired list is §2 Claim 1a.
- **Never** claim "we deleted her sentence and it found her standard" — we tested it; her note moves
  the line by £0–4, not £22. The **asymmetry** is the honest claim (£26 SPF defended to £54, £22
  cleanser only to £33).
- **Always** attach an alternative to a refusal. Her no is her most valuable output *because* it
  comes with what to buy instead.
- **Always** keep keys server-side. Done: `.env` gitignored, no key in any tracked file.
